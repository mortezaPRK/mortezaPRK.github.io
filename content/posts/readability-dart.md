Recently, while working on a side project of my own in Flutter, I needed to create a **clean** view of
a webpage. I knew about "Reader Mode" in browsers and remembered that Mozilla had a library specifically for this.

Initially I thought a dart package already exists, but I couldn't find something that I like, so I
used AI to rewrite the [Mozilla's Readability](https://github.com/mozilla/readability) and published it on
Pub.dev.

Check it out on [GitHub](https://github.com/mortezaPRK/readability) or [pub.dev](https://pub.dev/packages/reader_mode)!
